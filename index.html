1 · Connecticut rules

Connecticut has one of the stricter graduated licensing programs in the country, and the rules are unusual enough that guessing will get him suspended. This is the legal frame everything else sits inside.

The road to a license at 16 or 17

The one that catches families out: if no parent or legal guardian completes the two-hour parent training class, he cannot be licensed in Connecticut until he turns 18 — no matter how well he drives. Book it early.
